Our Genesis

An Independent Private Venture

Established in 2012, Finova Capital emerged out of a desire to detach investment management from proprietary bank product marketing. We recognized that true wealth management requires an absolute fiduciary alignment.

Today, we operate globally, providing private families, company executives, and charitable trusts with clear strategies across fixed income, real estate, and global public equities.

Certified SEC Fiduciary

We are legally bound to serve our clients' interests first. No secret commissions, no proprietary funds, no hidden incentives.

Regulatory Integrity

Ethical Codes & Compliance

We hold ourselves to rigorous regulatory metrics to guarantee uncompromised advisory security.

Fiduciary Fiduciary Standard

Under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, we operate under a strict fiduciary standard. This means our advisors are legally required to manage assets solely for your interest, detailing any potential conflicts, and keeping expenses as low as possible.

Independent Custodians

To guarantee client security, Finova Capital never directly handles or stores your raw cash. All client assets are held by independent third-party custodians (such as Charles Schwab or Fidelity Institutional), providing you with dual auditing trails.
